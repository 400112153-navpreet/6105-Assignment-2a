Delivery, collection and FAQs

Everything you need to know before you send an enquiry.

Delivery

We deliver across central Auckland and selected surrounding suburbs: Ponsonby, Grey Lynn, Parnell, Newmarket, Mount Eden, Epsom and Remuera. Delivery to other Auckland locations may be available by arrangement.

  • Standard delivery is generally available Monday to Saturday.
  • Allow at least one working day's notice.
  • Same-day requests may be possible but are not guaranteed.
  • Delivery charges vary depending on location.
  • Wedding, event and large corporate orders need additional notice.
